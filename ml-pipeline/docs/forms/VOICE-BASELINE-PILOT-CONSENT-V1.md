# Draft participant information and consent

This is a collection aid, not a legal instrument or ethics approval. A
responsible institutional/research-ethics reviewer must approve the final
wording, retention schedule, withdrawal process, and recruitment before anyone
is recorded.

## Study purpose

The Sahulat AI team is collecting fresh human speech to evaluate the
transcription accuracy of fixed, existing speech-recognition models for Urdu,
Urdu-English mixed speech, Sindhi, and Pashto. The study is a small research
pilot. It is not a production test and will not train or fine-tune models.

## What participation means

With your permission, the team records speech and prepares a human-verified
transcript. A pseudonymous speaker code is used in the evaluation files. The
recording may be processed locally by the listed ASR models and retained in a
restricted research workspace until the approved deletion date. Names,
signatures, contact information, and consent documents are stored separately
from recordings and transcripts.

Aggregate accuracy and timing results may be reported. A short de-identified
reference/transcription error example may be shared only if you separately
agree below and the study reviewer approves that specific excerpt. Raw audio
and signed consent evidence will not be publicly released under this pilot.

Participation is voluntary. Ask the study contact how to withdraw and until
what date withdrawal can be honored; explain that already published aggregate
results may not be retractable. Record the approved retention/deletion date on
the separate consent record. Do not record another person, private
conversation, account details, or information they would not expect to be used
in this study.

## Participant confirmation (complete on a separately stored form)

- I am at least 18 years old.
- I understand the purpose, audio handling, local model processing, retention,
  reporting, and withdrawal process described to me.
- I permit restricted private storage of these recordings for the stated
  research purpose.
- I permit local processing by the listed fixed ASR checkpoints for this pilot.
- I permit aggregate research metrics to be reported.
- Optional: I permit a specifically human-reviewed, de-identified transcript
  error excerpt to be used in a research report. (Yes / No)
- I do not permit public release of my audio under this pilot.

Participant signature/affirmation, date, retention/deletion date, and study
reviewer/contact details belong only on the separately stored consent record,
never in the recording manifest.
